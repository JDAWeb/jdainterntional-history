About these reports. Reports for 2001 and for 2006 to 2011 were not found in JDA's archive. The file once labeled as the 2001 report is the report for 2000. Photos are not altered: names are withheld, not faces. The files are otherwise as originally published. Financial statements and tax filings are not posted on this site.

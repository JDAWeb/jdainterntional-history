About these files

These are JDA's own publications, as originally issued, with a few changes to protect people. The names of local people, personal phone numbers and email addresses, and requests for support have been removed. Photos are shown as JDA published them; names are withheld, not faces. Figures are as JDA reported them at the time and may differ from later totals; see About the figures.

Joint Development Associates International

Community development in Uzbekistan, Afghanistan, and Iraqi Kurdistan, 1993 to 2025. JDA closed operations in October 2025.

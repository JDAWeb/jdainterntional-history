Notice of closure

As of October 2025, JDA International has closed operations after 32 years of service. This site preserves our history and achievements for reflection and reference.

From the founder

A Letter from Bob

Dear Friends,

With heartfelt gratitude, I write to inform you of the closure of Joint Development Associates International (JDA) after 32 remarkable years of service. Since our modest beginnings in Uzbekistan in 1993, JDA has been committed to transformational development, offering aid and support to communities in Uzbekistan, Afghanistan, and Iraq.

Our journey has been anchored by core values such as equality, humility, integrity, love and service, professional competence, accountability, and teachability. These principles have been the bedrock of our efforts to foster individual development, enhance economic and social conditions, and provide relief to those affected by extreme poverty, war, and natural disasters. None of this would have been achievable without the unwavering support of each one of you.

Read the full letter

We want to express our deepest appreciation for the hundreds of local staff members who have been the heart and soul of JDA's mission over these 32 years. Their dedication, cultural understanding, and tireless efforts have been instrumental in catalyzing transformational development within their communities. Through their work, we've seen communities embrace new agricultural technologies, adopt improved health practices, and gain access to clean water and education. Their ability to build trust, foster relationships, and effectively communicate our mission has been invaluable in creating lasting positive change.

To all our supporters, whether you joined us at the beginning or along the way, thank you for your invaluable contributions. Your support has been the driving force behind our mission across Central Asia and beyond. Together, we have provided food, water, shelter, and social and spiritual support to those most in need.

My wife and I wish to express our profound thanks to God for His guidance throughout this extraordinary journey. His wisdom has been instrumental in leading the hundreds of staff members who have worked tirelessly in Uzbekistan, Afghanistan, and Iraq. We are thankful for His blessings that have allowed us to serve others with compassion and dedication and for the many long-term friendships we have formed worldwide. We are especially grateful for the countless individuals who have come to faith during these years.

As we reflect on the lives we have touched, we feel a deep sense of accomplishment. While JDA may be concluding its operations, the spirit of service and empowerment will continue through the individuals and communities we have served. We will carry on with our work and service without the overhead of an organization.

Thank you once again for your incredible support over these 32 years. We are eternally grateful for your partnership in this journey.

With deepest gratitude and love,Bob Hedlund, Founder

By country

Results in each country

1993 to 2006 · Tashkent

Uzbekistan

Where JDA began: water near the Aral Sea, small loans and poultry for families in poverty, and care for leprosy-affected communities.

  • 1,000hand-pump wells drilled
  • 3,000small loans made, 85 percent of them to women, with a 95 percent repayment rate
  • 500doctors trained in leprosy treatment
  • 160families in the poultry program by 2003, in 12 villages
  • 600+children treated at the children's tuberculosis center in Nukus, which JDA helped repair
  • 46.5%drop in childhood diarrhea reported in communities using solar water disinfection (SODIS)

Highlights

  • 1996CEBIS computer training center opens in Jizzak
  • 1999Poultry program for widows and single mothers starts with seven families
  • 2003More than 60 new family businesses started in Muynak with micro-credit
  • 2005Ministry of Health approves expanding SODIS to five regions
More on Uzbekistan

2001 to 2019 · Mazar-i-Sharif

Afghanistan

Eighteen years in the north: emergency food, schools and homes, farm research and machinery, clean water, and hygiene training led by Afghan women educators.

  • 74wells with hand pumps, serving about 68,000 people
  • 30,000women trained in sanitation and hygiene
  • 6schools built in Afghanistan
  • 95staff on JDA's team in Afghanistan
  • 15,700men employed in food for work in the first two years of relief: roads, irrigation, brick making and hand-dug wells
  • 85,000+farmers worked with under USAID's RADP-North, 2014 to 2019

Highlights

  • 2003With Habitat for Humanity, more than 200 families build or rebuild their homes
  • 2004Sultan Ibrahim School dedicated, planned for 2,000 boys and girls
  • 2007OHT, JDA's sister company, begins importing two-wheel tractors, 494 in all
  • 2008859 farmers trained in melon fly control in three provinces
  • 2018More than 1,300 women have taken the BLiSS birth life saving skills course
More on Afghanistan

2017 to 2020 · Erbil

Iraqi Kurdistan

Helping families displaced by ISIS rebuild their livelihoods through the RIVAL program with ZOA.

  • 4,000+returnees, displaced people and host community members reached over two years
  • 790cash-for-work jobs in the first year of RIVAL, 140 of them for women
  • 547cash-for-work jobs in 2020, despite COVID-19 curfews
  • 300livestock grants in 2020, after sheep grants for 260 people, almost all women, in year one
  • 342people trained in greenhouse growing in 2020
  • 94%of women who received livestock had kept them

Highlights

  • 2017JDA registers in the Kurdistan Region and opens an office in Erbil
  • 2018RIVAL begins with ZOA, funded by the U.S. State Department (PRM)
  • 2019Workers renovate 1,500 meters of drainage channel in Beban village
  • 2020RIVAL meets its second-year goals, serving 1,828 people

How JDA worked

In each country JDA worked through local staff, hundreds of them over 32 years, who understood the communities they served. Afghan women educators led hygiene training in homes, local trainers taught farmers in their own fields, and loan officers in Uzbekistan worked with solidarity groups in their own villages. The aim was change that communities would carry on themselves, such as the two-wheel tractor services that farmers in northern Afghanistan went on to buy and run without subsidy.

JDA described its core values as equality, humility, integrity, love and service, professional competence, accountability, and teachability.
